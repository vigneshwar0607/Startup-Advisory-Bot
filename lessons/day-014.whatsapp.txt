*1. The Concept Recap*
Startups move through three phases: Setup (Entity, DPIIT), Governance (Founders' Agreement, SHA, Statutory Registers), and Scale (MIS, IP protection, and Funding). As a CA, you aren't just a tax filer; you are the "Due Diligence Officer" who ensures the startup doesn't collapse during an investor's audit.

*2. Practical CA Angle*
The primary service here is the *Pre-Funding Health Check*. You audit their ROC filings, IP assignments, and MIS accuracy.
- *Indicative Fee:* Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 75,000 depending on the volume of transactions and complexity of the cap table.

*3. Watch-outs*
- Founders often forget to assign IP from their personal names to the Company.
- Statutory registers (MGT-1, MBP-1) are usually blank in early-stage startups; this is a red flag for VCs.
- Mixing personal and business expenses makes the MIS useless for valuation.

*5. Digital Rupee today*
e₹ is a Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC), a direct liability of the RBI. Unlike Stablecoins (private fiat-pegged tokens) or Crypto (decentralized assets), e₹ is legal tender. Under the Income-tax Act, 1961, Crypto/Stablecoins are Virtual Digital Assets (VDAs) taxed at 30%, whereas e₹ is simply digital cash.
