*Startup Advisory — Day 9: Startup Compliance Calendar*

Early-stage founders focus on product and fundraising while letting basic filings slip. Late fees, de-activated DINs, and lost DPIIT benefits follow quickly. Pitching an integrated compliance calendar converts ad-hoc work into predictable monthly retainer revenue.

*Key Statutory Deadlines to Track:*
- *ROC / MCA:*
  - DPT-3 (Return of Deposits / Exempted Loans): 30 June
  - DIR-3 KYC (Director KYC): 30 September
  - AOC-4 (Financial Statements): within 30 days of AGM (usually 29/30 October)
  - MGT-7 / 7A (Annual Return): within 60 days of AGM (usually 29 November)
  - MSME-1: Half-yearly (30 April and 31 October) for outstanding payments over 45 days
- *TDS / TCS:*
  - Monthly tax deposit: 7th of next month (30 April for March)
  - Quarterly returns (Form 24Q, 26Q, 27Q): 31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May
- *GST:*
  - GSTR-1: 11th of every month (or 13th of month following quarter under QRMP)
  - GSTR-3B: 20th of every month (or 22nd/24th under QRMP)
  - GSTR-9 / 9C: 31 December
- *Labour Laws & DPIIT:*
  - PF & ESI remittances: 15th of every month
  - Labour Law Self-Certification: Startups recognized by DPIIT can self-certify compliance for 6 central labour laws (like Gratuity, Contract Labour, EPF, ESI) via the Shram Suvidha portal for up to 5 years, shielding them from routine inspections
  - DPIIT Portal: Keep registered office, director list, and cap table updated; watch the INR 100 crore turnover ceiling and 10-year incorporation window

*The CA Service & Pricing:*
Package this as a "Virtual Compliance Officer" retainer. Deliverables include a live dashboard, monthly filing receipts, and a quarterly compliance certificate for the Board.
_Indicative market fee:_ INR 8,000 to INR 25,000 per month for early-stage private limited companies (scaled by transaction volume and headcount).
